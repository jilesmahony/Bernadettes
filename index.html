Our Story

Auburn's most-loved
full-service salon

Since 2013, Bernadette's Salon has been a welcoming haven in the heart of Auburn, Indiana — the kind of place where you walk in as a client and leave as family. Our talented team of stylists and specialists deliver expert hair, nails, skincare, massage, and spa services with warmth and precision.

We're proud to be Auburn's home for Scruples color, Brazilian blowouts, a one-of-a-kind Head Spa experience, and our beloved Himalayan Salt Room. Whether you're treating yourself or planning a wedding party, we have you covered.
